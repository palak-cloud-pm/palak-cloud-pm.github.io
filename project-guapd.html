01 · The Problem

Brand-creator deals in India run on chaos

In India, brand-creator collaborations run through agencies, WhatsApp, DMs and bank transfers.

Neither side has transparency. Brands can’t easily see what they are paying for. Creators can’t easily see where a deal stands or when they will be paid.

How deals happen today
  • Agencies in the middle
  • Negotiation over WhatsApp and DMs
  • Payment by bank transfer
  • No shared record for either side

04 · AI Done Responsibly

AI on understanding. Code on ranking.

Claude-powered search turns a brand’s plain-language brief into structured filters. These are the product decisions that keep it trustworthy.

01
The model reads, it never ranks
The model only reads the brief. Ranking stays in code, so match reasons can never be invented.
02
Briefs are untrusted input
Every brief is treated as untrusted, with prompt-injection tests.
03
Verified vs self-reported, labelled
Verified creator stats and self-reported stats are labelled separately.
04
Creators can opt out
Creators can choose not to appear in AI search.
05
Restricted search for unapproved brands
Brands that are not yet approved get restricted search, to prevent abuse.

07 · What Didn’t Work & the Pivot

Creators came easily. Brands did not.

What didn’t work

Brands were slow to leave agencies

Creators joined easily. Brands were slow to leave the agency model.

The pivot

Narrower focus, managed shoots

We narrowed to beauty, fashion and D2C brands. We added managed content shoots, with a 2-sided money model: brands pay Guapd, Guapd pays creators.

That’s what started winning brands.

08 · What I Learned

Three takeaways

  • The hardest side of a marketplace sets your go-to-market. Creators were easy to win. Brands decided the strategy.
  • Keep AI on understanding. Keep ranking deterministic. That is how users learn to trust the results.
  • Build capability ahead. Switch it on when the business needs it. Feature flags and settings that change without a deploy made that possible.
